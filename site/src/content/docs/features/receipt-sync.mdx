A store trip usually shows up in Monarch as one lump sum. Monarch already splits Amazon and Target orders into what you bought. Retail receipt sync does the same for Walmart and Costco: it sends each purchase's receipt to Monarch, which matches it to the transaction and splits it.

<Screenshot name="receipt-sync" clip="receipt-sync" alt="The Sync retailer menu on Monarch's Transactions page, offering Walmart and Costco." />

## Using it

1. On **Transactions → Receipts**, click **Sync retailer** and choose a store.
2. The first time, Wingspan opens a page asking to read that store's site. Choose **Allow** for the store's site, then confirm in your browser's prompt. The sync starts on its own.
3. A tab opens at the store's purchase history, with a progress window over the page while Wingspan reads. Sign in there if you need to. Keep the tab open; it closes when it's done.
4. Next to **Sync retailer**, Monarch's tab shows how many purchases it has read and sent so far.
5. When it's done, a summary says how many receipts were sent. Choose **View receipts** to see them. Each one shows under **Receipts** once Monarch has matched it to its transaction and split it.

## What's sent

Each purchase becomes one receipt, laid out like the paper one: each item with its quantity and price, fees, discounts, tax and the total. If the items don't add up to what you were charged, an **Adjustment** line makes up the difference.

Wingspan never reads your name, email or address from the store, and never changes anything on its site.

## Which purchases

- The first sync covers the last 90 days. Later syncs pick up where the last one left off.
- Each purchase goes to Monarch once, whichever browser in the household syncs it, and not at all if Monarch already has its receipt.
- An order still being delivered is sent on a later sync, once it has a final total. Returns and canceled orders aren't sent.
- Monarch matches a receipt to a transaction with the same total. An order charged in more than one payment, like one shipped in parts, may not match.

## If a sync stops

The summary says why, with **Try again**:

- **Sign in to the store** in the tab that opened, then sync again.
- **The store asked to check you're not a robot**: finish that in the store's tab, then sync again.
- **The tab closed** or **took too long to load**: sync again.
- **The purchase history changed** in a way Wingspan can't read yet: Wingspan needs an update for that store.

The summary also says how many receipts were sent before it stopped. Syncing again never resends a receipt, so it's always safe to retry.

:::note
Monarch plans to sync more stores itself. Once Monarch supports a store Wingspan syncs, Wingspan deprecates and then removes its own sync for that store.
:::
