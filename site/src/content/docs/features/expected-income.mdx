Some income doesn't come in one steady amount. Client payouts from a booking app might be $145 one day and $580 the next, a few times a week. Monarch's recurring income needs one fixed amount, so this kind of income either goes missing from your plan or gets the last deposit's amount, which is usually too low.

Expected income is what you usually take in: say $430 a week. [Projected balances](/features/projected-balances/) adds it on each upcoming date, so checking doesn't look like it only goes down when money really is coming in.

<Screenshot name="expected-income" alt="Expected income's details: weekly, $430 expected, the deposits it matched, and the next one." />

## Using it

1. On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring → Add manually**, then **Income**.
2. Pick the merchant and a few of its deposits. Wingspan fills in the name, schedule and account from them.
3. Set **Expected amount** to what you usually take in each time, and save.

Expected income sits in Monarch's **Income** table. Any deposit that matches counts as received, whatever its size.

## How it's counted

- Every upcoming date adds the full expected amount. A small deposit doesn't lower the next one, since the amount is an average.
- Deposits already received are in your balance, so they aren't counted again.
- Income that didn't arrive isn't carried over as owed. It shows as not received, and the next date carries on as normal.
