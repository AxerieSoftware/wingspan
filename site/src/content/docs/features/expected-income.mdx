Some income varies. Client payouts from a booking app might be $145 one day and $580 the next, a few times a week. Monarch's recurring income needs one fixed amount, so this income either drops out of your plan or takes the last deposit's amount, usually too low.

Expected income is what you usually take in: say $430 a week. [Projected balances](/features/projected-balances/) adds it on each upcoming date, so checking doesn't look like it only goes down while money is coming in.

<Screenshot name="expected-income" alt="Expected income's details: weekly, $430 expected, the deposits it matched, and the next one." />

## Adding it

1. On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring → Add manually**, then set **Type** to **Income**.
2. Choose the **Merchant** and tick a few of its deposits. Wingspan fills in the rest from them.
3. Set **Expected amount** to your usual deposit and choose **Add recurring**.

## The options

- **Expected amount**: your average deposit, so it's fine if they vary.
- **Frequency**, **Next due**, **Transaction contains** and **Paid into** work like a [manual bill's](/features/manual-bills/#the-options).

## Reading it

Expected income sits in Monarch's **Income** table:

- **Expected** under the amount until it arrives, then the deposit's real amount.
- **Received Oct 3** once a deposit matches. Income due more than once a month shows **2 of 4 received**.
- **Not received Oct 3** when its date passed with no deposit.

## How it's counted

- Any matching deposit counts as received, whatever its size.
- Every upcoming date adds the full expected amount. A small deposit doesn't lower the next one.
- Missed income isn't carried over. The next date counts as normal.
