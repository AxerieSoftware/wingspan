When you review transactions, it helps to know whether the list is current. Monarch shows when each account last synced only on **Accounts**. Wingspan adds a small button to the top of **Transactions** that says when your accounts last synced, and opens each account's sync and a way to refresh them all.

<Screenshot name="sync-status" clip="sync-status" alt="Monarch's Transactions header with a 2h ago button before Search, open to a list of accounts: a card synced 19 days ago in orange, the rest 2 hours ago, and a Refresh all button." />

## Using it

- **2h ago** is when your accounts last synced. Choose it to see each one.
- An account in orange hasn't synced in over two days. Some cards, like Apple Card, sync only when their owner opens Monarch.
- **Needs attention** means a bank connection needs you to sign in again. Choose it to open Accounts and fix it. The button shows how many need attention.
- **Refresh all** asks your banks for new transactions, like **Refresh all** on Accounts. When it's done, choose **Reload** to see what came in.

## Good to know

- Accounts you track by hand, hidden accounts and accounts with syncing turned off aren't listed.
- Some banks take a few minutes to send new transactions after a refresh.
