Monarch's Recurring page shows what each bill costs, but not always when it's due. Wingspan adds a due date to every row, Monarch's included, and sorts each table by date, so bills appear in the order they'll leave checking.

<Screenshot name="due-dates" alt="Monarch's Recurring Expenses table with a due date under each bill, sorted soonest first." />

## Reading it

Open **Recurring** in Monarch. Under each name:

- **Due Oct 20** for a bill due once this month.
- **Next Oct 14** for one due more than once a month, like a weekly subscription, or **All paid** once this month's are done.
- In the **All** view, **Due the 20th** or **Due the last day** for a monthly bill.

Tables sort soonest first, with Wingspan's items mixed in by date.

## Setting the due day yourself

Monarch guesses a bill's timing from its last payment, so a bill paid early or late can show the wrong day.

1. Open the item on **Recurring**.
2. In its details, set **Due day** to its real day of the month. Days 29 to 31 fall on the last day in shorter months.
3. To go back to Monarch's date, choose **Automatic**.

Your day is used on Recurring and in [Projected balances](/features/projected-balances/), for everyone in your household.

## Good to know

- **Due day** shows for items due once a month or less often.
- It only moves the date. The amount and whether it's paid still come from Monarch.
