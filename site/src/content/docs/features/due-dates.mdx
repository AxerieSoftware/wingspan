Monarch's Recurring page shows what your bills and subscriptions cost, but not always when each one is due. Wingspan adds a due date to every row, Monarch's own included, and sorts each table by date, so you see bills in the order they'll come out of checking.

<Screenshot name="due-dates" alt="Monarch's Recurring Expenses table with a due date under each bill, sorted soonest first." />

## Using it

Open **Recurring** in Monarch. Each row shows its due date, soonest first.

Monarch works out a bill's timing from when money left last time, so a bill paid early or late can look like it's due on the wrong day. Open the item and set its due day yourself. Wingspan uses your day from then on, in Recurring and in [Projected balances](/features/projected-balances/).
