Monarch's Cash Flow shows where your money went. Projected balances shows what's coming: checking day by day ahead, with the paychecks, bills, card payments and everyday spending you can expect. You see a low point before you hit it.

It sits on **Cash Flow**, under Monarch's chart. Pick **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly** at the top of Cash Flow to look about a month, 90 days or a year ahead.

<Screenshot name="projected-balances" clip="projected-balances" alt="Projected balances on Cash Flow: checking day by day ahead, free cash today and the monthly surplus." />

## Free cash today

How much you could move out of checking today to spend, save or pay a card down early. Every bill still gets paid, every card's minimum is met, and the amount you keep in checking stays untouched for the weeks ahead and through the next payday.

If you budget toward goals in Monarch, free cash splits into **to goals**, already promised to them, and **truly free**. It also appears in Recurring's month summary, next to your bills.

<Screenshot name="free-cash" alt="Recurring's month summary with the Statements line and Free cash today, linking to Projected balances." />

## Reading the card

- **Free cash today**: what's safe to move out of checking. Hover it for what it covers.
- **Short from Oct 14**, in red, replaces it when checking would drop below what you keep, showing the gap at its lowest. There's no free cash until that's covered.
- **Monthly surplus**: what an average month leaves after income, bills and everyday spending. Below $0, you fall behind a little each month.
- **Credit left**: the lowest your available credit gets, and when a card would max out.
- **Checking runs out**: the first day checking would go below $0, or **Not by Nov 10** if it doesn't.
- **+$1,782.64 net by Nov 10**, next to the title: how much checking grows or shrinks over the range.

### The chart

- The solid line is **Checking**, red where it's below what you keep. The dashed line, **After cards**, is checking minus what the cards owe.
- Green marks are paydays, orange marks are card payments, and red or yellow marks flag a problem, like a card maxing out.
- Hover a day for its balance and what came in or went out.

## Setting it up

On **Cash Flow**, open **Edit cash and cards**, or use the pencil next to Free cash today on Recurring.

<Screenshot name="cash-and-cards" alt="The Cash and cards dialog, choosing checking accounts, reserves, cards and how much to keep in checking." />

- **Cash accounts**: mark each as **Checking**, where bills and card payments come from, a **Reserve** you'd draw on if checking ran low, or **Not counted**.
- **Always keep**: a cushion that stays in checking. Free cash never counts it.
- **Keep free cash safe for**: 30, 60 or 90 days ahead, or until the next paycheck if that's later.
- **Cards counted as debt**: when checking runs low, the plan borrows on these cards from the top of the list, then draws on reserves.

A warning next to a card means Monarch is missing its credit limit, APR or minimum payment. Add it in the card's details in Monarch.

If you use Monarch's businesses, the household and each business have their own settings. Pick one under **Settings for**.

## What it counts

- Monarch's recurring income and bills, on the due days you've set
- Wingspan's [bills](/features/manual-bills/) and [card payments](/features/card-payments/)
- [Expected income](/features/expected-income/), at what you usually take in
- Everyday spending, at your usual pace from recent months
- Each card's statement, paid as far as checking allows, with anything left over carrying interest
- Bills that are already late

Money going out counts as soon as it's pending, and money coming in once it lands, so free cash never runs ahead of what's there.

## Good to know

- A card with no due date is assumed due in 30 days. Add a [card payment](/features/card-payments/) to set it.
- If Monarch also tracks one of your Wingspan bills as a recurring bill, it counts once, from Monarch's.
- **Couldn't refresh from Monarch** means you're seeing the last numbers that loaded. Choose **Try again**.

:::note
Monarch Plus forecasts years ahead. Projected balances looks up to a year ahead, day by day. If Monarch's forecasting adds that, Wingspan will remove Projected balances.
:::
