With Monarch's Plus plan, you can put accounts under a business. Monarch lets you filter some pages by business, but only one page at a time, and the choice resets when you open a new tab. It's easy to end up reading business spending as household spending.

Wingspan adds a switcher to the top of Monarch's sidebar. Pick the household or a business, and supported pages show only that workspace's money: its spending, income, bills, card payments and [free cash](/features/projected-balances/#free-cash-today). Your choice stays until you switch again, in every tab and after a restart.

<Screenshot name="workspaces" alt="The workspace switcher at the top of Monarch's sidebar, choosing between the household and a business." />

Some pages can't be filtered by business in Monarch. Those say so in their header, so you always know when you're looking at everything together.

Each browser keeps its own choice, so one person can work in the business while another stays in the household. The switcher only appears for households with a business set up in Monarch, and you can turn it off in Monarch's Settings, under Wingspan.
