With Monarch's Plus plan, you can put accounts under a business. Monarch lets you filter some pages by business, but only one page at a time, and the choice resets when you open a new tab. It's easy to end up reading business spending as household spending.

Wingspan adds a switcher to the top of Monarch's sidebar. Pick the household or a business, and supported pages show only that workspace's money: its spending, income, bills, card payments and [free cash](/features/projected-balances/#free-cash-today). Your choice stays until you switch again, in every tab and after a restart.

<Screenshot name="workspaces" alt="The workspace switcher at the top of Monarch's sidebar, choosing between the household and a business." />

## Using it

1. Click the switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Household** or a business. The page reloads in that workspace.

**Manage businesses**, in the same menu, opens Monarch's settings for your businesses.

## What follows the workspace

- Pages Monarch can filter by business open filtered to your workspace. You can still change Monarch's filter on the page.
- On **Recurring**, which Monarch can't filter, Wingspan hides the bills and income of other workspaces and redoes the month's totals for yours.
- Wingspan's own features, like free cash and [Interest cost](/features/interest-cost/), count only the workspace's accounts.
- Pages that can't be filtered say **Not filtered by workspace** in their header, so you always know when you're looking at everything together.

A bill or card payment belongs to the workspace of the account it's paid from. One with no account belongs to the household.

## Turning it off

In Monarch's **Settings**, under **Wingspan**, open **General**. Under **Business workspaces**, switch off **Keep the household and businesses separate**. Pages then open as Monarch opens them, and Monarch's own filters still work.

## Good to know

- The switcher only appears for households with a business set up in Monarch.
- Each browser keeps its own choice, so one person can work in the business while another stays in the household.
- If a business is deleted in Monarch, its workspace falls back to the household.
