Monarch marks new transactions as needing review, but you only see how many once you open Transactions and filter for them. Wingspan puts that count on **Transactions** in the sidebar, like the count Monarch shows on Recurring, so you can tell from any page when there's something to check.

<Screenshot name="transactions-to-review" alt="Transactions in Monarch's sidebar with an orange 3 for the transactions to review." />

## Good to know

- The count follows your [workspace](/features/workspaces/): the household's transactions, or a business's.
- It's hidden when nothing needs review.
- After you review transactions, the count catches up within a minute as you move between pages.
- Monarch's own settings decide which transactions need review. Wingspan only counts them.
