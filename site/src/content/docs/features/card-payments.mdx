Monarch's **Statements** card on Recurring, meant for credit card and loan payments, says "Coming soon". Wingspan fills it in with each card's statement, due date and whether checking can pay it that day.

<Screenshot name="card-payments" clip="card-payments" alt="Recurring's Statements card filled in with three credit cards, each with its due date, statement amount and whether checking can pay it." />

## Adding a card

1. On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring → Add manually**, then set **Type** to **Card payment**.
2. Choose the **Card** and its **Due day**, then **Add recurring**.

For a card Monarch doesn't have, like a store card you pay from checking, choose **Not in Monarch** and fill in:

- **Payment description contains**: text on its payments in checking, often the card's last four digits.
- **Typical payment**: what you usually pay, used as the amount due.

<Screenshot name="card-payment-editor" alt="Add recurring with Card payment chosen, for a card not in Monarch: name, due day, payment description and typical payment." />

## Reading it

Each row shows what's due on the card's statement and its due date. When the statement is smaller than the card's balance, the line under it says **Statement · $1,250.40 balance**. Below that is what checking can pay that day:

- **Can pay in full**, in green.
- **Can pay $300**, in amber: part of it.
- **Can pay $20 of $35 min**, in red: not even the minimum.
- **~$35 est. min** means Monarch has no minimum for the card, so Wingspan estimates one: 1% of the statement plus a month's interest, at least $35 (2% of the balance when Monarch has no APR). Add the real minimum to the card's details in Monarch to use it instead.

These come from [Projected balances](/features/projected-balances/), after bills due earlier and the amount you keep in checking. The footer adds up what's due on this month's statements, or says **All paid**.

Monarch doesn't share statement dates, so Wingspan works out each statement from the card's balance and assumes it closed about 25 days before it's due. Once a payment is matched, it allows for purchases made just before the close that posted after it.

## When a statement counts as paid

- Only once nothing is left on it. A partial payment leaves it owed, and overdue after the due date.
- A refund on the card isn't a payment, and a payment the bank returns doesn't count.
- An unpaid statement folds into the next one instead of being owed twice.
- A card that owes $0 in Monarch counts as paid.
- If a statement you paid still shows as owed, choose **Mark as paid** in its row's menu, or **Choose payment…** to pick the payment. **Mark Oct 1 as not paid** undoes either.

## Good to know

- **Balance** under the amount means it's the card's balance in Monarch. **Typical** means it's your typical payment.
- **Unknown** with **Set a typical payment** means a card not in Monarch has no typical payment yet. **No balance from Monarch** means Monarch hasn't reported the card's balance.
- **Edit cash and cards** on Cash Flow sets which accounts are checking and how much to keep there.

:::note
Once Monarch releases its Statements card, Wingspan will remove card payments.
:::
