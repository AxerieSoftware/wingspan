Monarch shows your card balances, but its **Statements** card, meant for credit card and loan payments, says "Coming soon". Wingspan fills it in: each card's statement, when it's due, and whether checking can pay it on that day.

<Screenshot name="card-payments" alt="Recurring's Statements card filled in with three credit cards, each with its due date, statement amount and whether checking can pay it." />

## What it tells you

- Each card's statement balance and due date.
- Whether checking can pay it in full on that day, only part of it, or not even the minimum, while keeping the amount you set aside in checking.
- The minimum payment, from Monarch, or estimated by Wingspan when Monarch doesn't have one.
- When a statement is paid. It counts as paid only once nothing is left on it.
- What's still owed across your cards this month, and whether checking covers it.

A card you pay but haven't linked in Monarch can still be tracked, by the payments that leave checking for it.

## Using it

1. On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring → Add manually**, then **Card payment**, and pick the card.
2. On **Cash Flow**, open **Edit cash and cards** to choose which accounts are checking and how much to keep there.

:::note
When Monarch releases its Statements card, Wingspan's card payments will be deprecated and removed.
:::
