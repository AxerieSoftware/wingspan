Halfway through a month, Cash Flow's bars only show what has posted. A quarterly insurance bill or an annual subscription due next week isn't there yet, so the month can look better than it will end.

Month-end projection extends this month's bars to where the month is headed, using the same bills, income and everyday spending as [Projected balances](/features/projected-balances/).

<Screenshot name="month-end-projection" clip="month-end-projection" alt="Cash Flow's monthly bars with October's extended, dashed, past what has posted so far, and its tooltip adding projected income and expenses for the month." />

## Where it shows

On **Cash Flow**, with **Monthly** picked:

- This month's income and expense bars get a dashed extension out to their projected month-end totals.
- Hover this month's bars, and Monarch's summary adds **Projected income** and **Projected expenses**: what's posted so far plus what's still expected by the last day of the month.

## How it's counted

- **Income** adds the paychecks and other income still due this month.
- **Expenses** add the bills still due this month, on checking or a card, plus everyday spending at your usual pace for each day left after today.
- Card payments and money moved between your accounts aren't counted, since they aren't income or spending.
- It follows the same accounts and workspace as Projected balances.

## Good to know

- A projection taller than the chart's scale stops at the chart's edge. The totals when you hover are still exact.
- Quarterly and Yearly views don't show it.
- Bills and income on accounts Projected balances doesn't follow, like a brokerage, aren't in the projection.
