Monarch's sidebar lists every page, whether you use it or not. Hide the ones you don't, and the sidebar shows only what you open. You can hide any item in it, the buttons at the bottom too.

<Screenshot name="sidebar-items" alt="The Sidebar section of Wingspan's settings, with a checkbox for each item in Monarch's sidebar and two of them unchecked." />

## Using it

1. In Monarch's **Settings**, under **Wingspan**, open **General**.
2. Under **Sidebar**, uncheck what you want hidden. It disappears from the sidebar right away.

Hidden items stay hidden as Monarch loads, so they don't flash in and disappear. A hidden page still opens from a link or a bookmark. Settings stays at the top of the sidebar, so you can always get back to show an item again.

Each browser keeps its own choice, so everyone in the household can set up the sidebar their way.
