Monarch counts loan payments as spending. A month you put an extra $1,000 toward a student loan looks the same as a month you spent $1,000, and your savings rate drops even though you're better off.

Loans paid down shows how much of those payments lowered what you owe. It sits after Monarch's **Savings rate**: the amount, with its share of your income beside the name.

## Where it shows

- **Cash Flow**, for the month, quarter or year you pick.
- **Reports**, on the **Cash flow** tab, for the dates you choose. The share uses the total income Reports shows, with your filters applied. An all-time report has no start date, so the card waits until you pick dates.

Hover **Loans paid down** for the dates it covers and how much each loan went down.

## How it's counted

- It's how much your loan balances went down over the period (through today, if the period is still going): what you paid minus interest added. It's negative when interest or new borrowing added more.
- A loan you track by hand only changes when you enter a balance, so once its payment is linked under **Pays toward**, Wingspan counts its payments in the period minus estimated interest instead.
- A loan paid off and closed during the period counts down to $0.
- A loan added during the period isn't counted, so new debt doesn't show up as a negative paydown.
- With Monarch's businesses, it counts loans for whatever the page shows: Household, a business, or both. Hidden loans aren't counted.

## Good to know

- A loan you track by hand without a linked payment counts once you update its balance. [Link its payment](/features/interest-cost/#keeping-a-manual-loan-current) so each month counts what you paid that month.
- The card is hidden when no loan shown had a balance at the start of the period.
- **Unavailable** means Monarch's loan balances didn't load. Wingspan tries again a minute later.
