Setting a budget is easier when you know what a category usually costs. Monarch shows a six-month average while you budget, which misses quarterly bills and once-a-year costs. Spending averages adds longer and shorter ones where you'd look for them.

<Screenshot name="spending-averages" clip="spending-averages" alt="Monarch's budget History for Groceries with Spent last month and Monthly average, and Wingspan's 3-month, 12-month and year-to-date averages and Spent this year under them." />

## On Budget

Click a category's planned amount on **Budget**. Monarch's **History** opens with **Spent last month** and a six-month **Monthly average**. Under those, Wingspan adds two more rows:

- **3-month average**: the last three full months.
- **12-month average**: the last twelve full months.
- **Year-to-date average**: this year's full months so far. It's left out in January, before a month has ended.
- **Spent this year**: everything so far this year, this month included. For an income category, **Earned this year**.

Click an average to use it as the category's budget, as you can with Monarch's own. Press **Enter** to save it.

## On a category's page

Click a category's name on **Budget**, or a category in **Cash Flow**'s breakdown, to open its page. Its **Summary**, beside the transactions, adds **Average per month** under Monarch's **Average transaction**:

<Screenshot name="category-average" alt="A category's Summary with Wingspan's Average per month between Monarch's Average transaction and Total spending." />

- For a quarter or year you pick on the chart, its spending divided by its months. Picking 2025 in **Yearly** gives 2025's monthly average.
- For this year, its spending so far divided by the months so far, this one included.
- For all time, its spending divided by the months since its first transaction.

## Good to know

- A month with nothing spent in the category counts as $0, so an annual bill spreads across the year.
- The averages count what Monarch's budget counts in the category, refunds included.
- A month, week or day picked on a category's chart has no average per month.
