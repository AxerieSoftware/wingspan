You can pay yourself back from an HSA for a medical expense any time after you paid it, even years later, as long as the HSA was open by then. If you pay out of pocket and leave the HSA invested, you need a running list of what you haven't reimbursed. HSA reimbursements keeps that list on your HSA's account page in Monarch, built from a tag you put on each expense.

<Screenshot name="hsa-reimbursements" clip="hsa-reimbursements" alt="The HSA reimbursements card on an HSA's page in Monarch: $597.85 to reimburse across two expenses, one without a receipt, and a Reimbursed section." />

## Using it

1. In [Monarch's tag settings](https://app.monarch.com/settings/tags), make two tags, like **HSA – Reimburse** and **HSA – Reimbursed**.
2. When you pay a medical expense yourself, tag it **HSA – Reimburse**. Attach the receipt or EOB and add notes, like who it was for.
3. Open your HSA in **Accounts**. The card shows what you can still reimburse, oldest expense first, and flags any without a receipt.
4. When the HSA pays you back, swap the expense's tag for **HSA – Reimbursed**. It moves into the **Reimbursed** section, which appears once there's something in it and keeps its own total.

## Choosing the tags

Tags with "HSA" and "reimburse" or "reimbursed" in their names are picked up on their own. Otherwise, choose **Choose tags** on the card:

- **Expenses to reimburse**: the tag you put on expenses you paid yourself.
- **Reimbursed expenses**: the tag you swap in once the HSA pays you back, or **None** if you'd rather remove the first tag.

Everyone in the household sees the same list.

<Screenshot name="hsa-tags" alt="The HSA tags dialog, choosing the tag for expenses to reimburse and the tag for reimbursed expenses." />

## Reading it

- The total at the top is what you can still pay yourself back.
- **without a receipt**, in amber, counts expenses with nothing attached in Monarch, each marked **No receipt**.
- Click an expense to open it in Monarch, where you attach receipts and change tags.
- **Reimbursed** lists what you've been paid back for, newest first.

## On Budget

Medical bills paid from your HSA still count toward Monarch's budget, so a big one can make **Flexible** look overspent when your everyday budget is fine. On **Budget**, each category and group row with HSA spending that month gets a line under its name, like **$185 paid from HSA**.

<Screenshot name="hsa-budget" alt="Medical on Monarch's Budget with $64 paid from HSA under its name." />

It counts spending on your HSA accounts, plus expenses tagged to reimburse from your HSA or already reimbursed. Monarch's numbers stay as they are, so take that amount off the row's spending to see how the rest of your budget is doing.

## Good to know

- The card shows on accounts whose type in Monarch is Health Savings Account (HSA).
- Expenses from any account and any year count. A tagged refund lowers the total.
- The tags, receipts and notes stay on your transactions in Monarch, with or without Wingspan. Keep receipts for as long as you might be asked about a withdrawal.
- Wingspan only reads your transactions here; you change the tags in Monarch.
- How you categorize the HSA's repayment doesn't change the list. A positive Health & Medical amount offsets that spending in Monarch's reports for the month it arrives; a transfer leaves your medical spending at its full amount.
