You can pay yourself back from an HSA for a medical expense any time after you paid it, even years later, as long as the HSA was open by then. Many people pay out of pocket and leave the HSA invested, so they need a running list of what they haven't reimbursed yet. HSA reimbursements keeps that list on your HSA's account page in Monarch, built from a tag you put on each expense.

<Screenshot name="hsa-reimbursements" alt="The HSA reimbursements card on an HSA's page in Monarch: $597.85 to reimburse across two expenses, one without a receipt, and a Reimbursed section." />

## Using it

1. In Monarch's settings, make two tags, like **HSA – Reimburse** and **HSA – Reimbursed**.
2. When you pay a medical expense yourself, tag it **HSA – Reimburse**. Attach the receipt or EOB to the transaction and add notes, like who it was for.
3. Open your HSA in **Accounts**. The card shows what you can still reimburse, oldest expense first, and flags any without a receipt.
4. When the HSA pays you back, swap the expense's tag for **HSA – Reimbursed**. It moves into the **Reimbursed** section, which keeps its own total.

Tags with "HSA" and "reimburse" or "reimbursed" in their names are picked up on their own. Otherwise, choose them with **Choose tags** on the card.

## Good to know

- The card shows on accounts whose type in Monarch is Health Savings Account (HSA).
- The tags, receipts and notes live on your transactions in Monarch, so the record stays there, in Monarch's phone app and exports too, with or without Wingspan. Keep receipts for as long as you might be asked about a withdrawal.
- Expenses from any year are counted, not just recent ones.
- Wingspan only reads your transactions here; you change the tags in Monarch.
- How you categorize the deposit when the HSA pays you back doesn't change the list. A positive Health & Medical amount offsets that spending in Monarch's reports for the month it arrives; a transfer leaves your medical spending at its full amount.
