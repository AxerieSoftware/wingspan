Interest is easy to lose track of, because on a loan you track by hand it never shows up as a transaction. Interest cost puts a number on it: what your debts cost each day, month and year at today's balances.

## Where it shows

- **Cash Flow** and **Reports**: **Interest a month**, after [Loans paid down](/features/loans-paid-down/), so you can see what you paid down next to what interest adds back. Hover it for the cost a day and a year, and each debt's rate and share.
- **Accounts**: the **Loans** and **Credit Cards** groups say what their debts cost a month.
- **Each loan's page**: an **Interest** card with the cost a day, a month and a year.

## How it's counted

- A loan counts when Monarch has an interest rate for it. Set it in the loan's account settings in Monarch. Without one, the loan's Interest card says so.
- A card counts only when it was charged interest in about the last two statements. A card you pay in full each month costs no interest, so it isn't counted.
- A card with no APR in Monarch gets the rate its last interest charge works out to, at most 36%.
- A year's interest is the balance times the rate, a day's is a 365th of that and a month's a 12th. Most loans and carried card balances add interest daily, so this is close to what you're charged.
- It counts the debts of whatever the page shows: Household, a business, or both. Hidden accounts aren't counted.

## Keeping a manual loan current

For a loan you track by hand in Monarch, Wingspan works out the new balance after each payment.

### 1. Add the loan's payment on Recurring

On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring → Add manually**, then set **Type** to **Loan payment**. Choose the **Loan**, then the **Merchant** and a few of its payments, like a [manual bill](/features/manual-bills/#adding-a-bill). It shows with your bills, owing its monthly amount.

### 2. Check the estimate after a payment

Open the loan's page. The **Interest** card shows the balance you last entered and an estimate for today: that balance, plus daily interest since, minus payments since. Without a Loan payment, it adds interest only.

### 3. Update the balance

Choose **Update balance**. It starts at the estimate. Save it, or enter your servicer's amount if it's different. Monarch's page reloads to show the new balance.

Wingspan changes the balance only when you save. Your servicer's amount can be a little lower, since most charge interest on the principal only.

## Good to know

- **Update balance** appears for loans you track by hand. A pending payment counts once it posts.
- **Monarch has no balance for this loan yet** means the loan needs a first balance entered in Monarch.
