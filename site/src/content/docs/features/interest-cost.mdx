On a loan you track by hand, interest never shows up as a transaction, so it's easy to lose track of. Interest cost shows what your debts cost each day, month and year at today's balances.

## Where it shows

- **Cash Flow** and **Reports**: **Interest a month**, after [Loans paid down](/features/loans-paid-down/), so you can see what you paid down next to what interest adds back. Hover it for the cost a day and a year, and each debt's rate and share. It uses today's balances, so it's hidden for periods that have ended.
- **Accounts**: the **Loans** and **Credit Cards** groups say what their debts cost a month.
- **Each loan's page**: an **Interest** card with the cost a month, a day and a year.

## How it's counted

- A loan counts when Monarch has an interest rate for it. Set it in the loan's account settings in Monarch. Without one, the loan's Interest card says so.
- A card counts only when it was charged interest in about the last two statements, so one you pay in full each month isn't counted.
- A card with no APR in Monarch gets the rate its last interest charge works out to, at most 36%.
- A year's interest is the balance times the rate, a day's is a 365th of that and a month's a 12th. Most loans and carried card balances add interest daily, so this is close to what you're charged.
- It counts the debts of whatever the page shows: Household, a business, or both. Hidden accounts aren't counted.

## Keeping a manual loan current

For a loan you track by hand in Monarch, Wingspan works out the new balance after each payment.

### 1. Link the loan's payment on Recurring

On **Recurring**, open the loan's payment (the recurring expense Monarch already tracks) and choose the loan under **Pays toward**. If Monarch doesn't track the payment yet, add it with **Add recurring → Add manually**, leaving **Type** on **Recurring merchant**. Wingspan's own bills can't pay toward a loan.

<Screenshot name="pays-toward" alt="Monarch's Auto Loan expense on Recurring, with Pays toward set to the Auto Loan." />

### 2. Check the estimate after a payment

Open the loan's page. The **Interest** card shows the balance you last entered and an estimate for today: daily interest on what's still owed, with each payment covering the interest so far before the principal. Without an interest rate in Monarch, it's the balance minus payments. Until its payment is linked, it adds interest only.

### 3. Update the balance

Once a linked payment posts, Wingspan reminds you to update the balance:

- **Transactions**: the payment's details say which loan it goes toward, with an **Update balance** button. You see it while reviewing the payment.
- **Accounts**: the loan's row says **Payment posted, update balance**, and **Accounts** in the sidebar counts the loans to update.
- **The loan's page**: the **Interest** card says how much posted since the balance was entered.

<Screenshot name="payment-update-banner" alt="A $340 payment's transaction details, saying it goes toward the Auto Loan, with an Update balance button." />

Choose **Update balance** there or on the loan's page. Enter your servicer's amount, or choose **Use estimate** to fill in Wingspan's. **Save to Monarch** saves it as the loan's balance for today. Monarch's page reloads to show it, and the reminders go away.

<Screenshot name="update-balance" alt="The Update balance dialog, with Use estimate offering Wingspan's estimate of $8,788.00." />

Wingspan changes the balance only when you save. Your servicer's amount is the one to trust if it differs.

## Good to know

- **Update balance** appears for loans you track by hand. A pending payment counts once it posts.
- If you merge recurring items in Monarch, choose the loan under **Pays toward** again on the merged item.
- **Monarch has no balance for this loan yet** means the loan needs a first balance entered in Monarch.
