Some bills don't follow a pattern Monarch can spot, like rent paid by check that the landlord cashes whenever they get to it. Monarch only sees each payment once it clears, so a late check can look like two bills one month and none the next.

A manual bill keeps the due date you set and stays unpaid until its payment clears, however late. Until then it counts as money leaving checking, so [free cash](/features/projected-balances/#free-cash-today) never treats it as yours to spend.

## Adding a bill

1. On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring → Add manually**, then set **Type** to **Bill**.
2. Choose the **Merchant**, then tick a few of its past payments. Wingspan fills in the rest from them.
3. Check the fields below and choose **Add recurring**.

## The options

- **Amount**: what the bill usually costs. A payment counts if it's within 10% or $5 of this, whichever is more.
- **Any amount**: for a bill that changes every time, like a utility.
- **Frequency** and **Next due**: weekly up to yearly, twice a month on two set days, or **Custom** for every so many weeks, months or years.
- **Transaction contains**: text from the payment's description, like the landlord's name. Wingspan uses it to find the payment.
- **Paid from**: the account or card it comes out of. **Any account** works unless the same text shows up in payments from other accounts. If you use [workspaces](/features/workspaces/), set it so the bill lands in the right one; a bill on **Any account** belongs to the household.

If another bill already matches the same payments, the editor says so. Set **Paid from** or a narrower description to tell them apart.

To change or stop a bill, use **Edit** or **Remove** in its menu on Recurring.

## Reading it

- **Due Oct 20** under the name, or **Next Oct 20** for a bill due more than once a month.
- **Paid Oct 18** once a payment matches. A bill due more than once a month shows **2 of 4 paid**; open the arrow beside it to see each due date.
- **Not paid yet** for the first few days after the due date, since payments often post late. Then **Overdue Oct 20**. An unpaid bill from an earlier month shows **Owed since** that date for up to six months.
- If Wingspan missed the payment, like a check with a different description, use **Choose payment…** in its menu to pick the transaction. If you paid some other way, like cash, choose **Mark as paid**. **Mark Oct 1 as not paid** undoes either.
- Its details show each payment, and **No payment found** for any due date it missed.

## Good to know

- A payment counts from a few days before the due date, however late it is.
- Each payment pays one due date, oldest first. When two bills could take the same payment on the same day, the one closest to its amount gets it.
- Changing the amount, schedule or match text starts the bill fresh. Moving only the due date keeps what's owed.
