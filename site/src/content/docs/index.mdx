## How it fits with Monarch

- **Built into Monarch.** Wingspan's additions sit on the Monarch pages you already use and look like Monarch, with a small Wingspan mark so you can tell them apart.
- **Only what Monarch doesn't do yet.** When Monarch releases the same feature, Wingspan's version is deprecated and removed.
- **Your data stays with you.** Wingspan saves to your own Monarch account. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking.
